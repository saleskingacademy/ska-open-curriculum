# Sales King Academy Complete Book

Author and editorial owner: Robert Kaleb Long, Sales King Academy LLC. All rights reserved.

**Scope (author's direction):** teach modern business, sales, marketing and AI integration from absolute
beginner to advanced practitioner. Technology-education first. Sales King Academy appears only as a case
study where a real, working integration demonstrates a concept; never credentials, secrets or unsupported
claims. Language decipherment and cipher research are excluded.

**Each subject moves:** definition, mental model, fundamentals, applied skill, implementation, integration,
failure modes, security and governance, assessment, advanced practice. Written sequentially; completed
material is never restarted. Fast-moving technology is researched before claims are made, and every claim
is labelled established, demonstrated research, in development, credible possibility, or speculation.

**Built on:** the real-subject map (`REAL_SUBJECTS.md`), the textbook standard (`TEXTBOOK_STANDARD.md`),
the 8-level ladder (`LEVELS.md`) and Education-16 addressing (`EDU16.md`). Part-to-subject map with
addresses: `map/book_parts.json`.

Written so far: Part 13 opening chapters, *AI Foundations for Business* and *AI Tools and Platforms*
(`knowledge/sales_king_academy/`), each 8,000+ words and 100/100 on the chapter standard.
