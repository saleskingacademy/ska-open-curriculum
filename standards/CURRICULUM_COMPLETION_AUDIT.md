# Curriculum Completion Audit and Deployment Plan

Generated from the current public catalogue and the SKA Zero-to-Frontier Curriculum Standard.

## Baseline
The catalogue contains 369 real subjects. The existing catalogue defines a nominal full-textbook target of at least 120,000 words and 12+ chapters per real subject. Current catalogue total is 4,290,895 words with 40,021,105 words listed as still needed under that legacy quantitative target.

Only two subjects currently reach at least 60,000 words (half of the 120,000-word quantitative target):
- Professional Selling — 73,619 words
- Marketing Analytics and Growth — 60,535 words

Word count alone does not establish educational equivalence or completion. Under the new standard, completion additionally requires prerequisite closure, coherent sequencing, lesson-level instruction, worked examples, practice, verified solutions, semantically valid assessments, remediation, cumulative mastery, computational treatment where relevant, and AI-augmented/frontier treatment.

## Deployment rule
Expansion is competency-first, not word-count-first. Existing content is preserved unless incorrect, duplicated, misplaced, unsafe, or low-quality generated material. Each subject receives:
1. canonical prerequisite graph;
2. zero-to-frontier scope;
3. modules and lessons in dependency order;
4. worked examples and applications;
5. formative assessment and remediation;
6. cumulative mastery examinations/projects;
7. computational and AI-augmented modules where relevant;
8. provenance and epistemic labeling;
9. Education-16 placement;
10. Symbol256 rebuild only after content/taxonomy validation.

## Priority waves
Wave A — foundational dependencies: mathematics, English/literacy, critical thinking, communication, study skills, computing/programming, statistics, research methods.
Wave B — core academic disciplines: physical/life sciences, history, economics, psychology, sociology, philosophy, literature, business foundations.
Wave C — advanced technical/professional: AI/ML, software engineering, cybersecurity, cloud, engineering, finance/accounting, law, health, education.
Wave D — specializations, trades, arts, languages and services.
Wave E — computational, AI-augmented and frontier extensions across every applicable discipline.

## Quality gates
A subject is not COMPLETE because it has reached 120,000 words. It is COMPLETE only after:
- every declared unit exists;
- prerequisite dependencies are satisfied;
- beginner material assumes no hidden subject knowledge;
- advanced material reaches the declared competency ceiling;
- exercises and solutions have been checked;
- quizzes measure understanding rather than template artifacts;
- cumulative assessment measures transfer;
- taxonomy and cross-links are correct;
- factual claims that require currency are sourced/versioned;
- established knowledge is separated from SKA-defined or experimental research.

## Immediate finding
The repository has broad topical coverage but remains far from the declared full-textbook target across almost the entire catalogue. Expansion should therefore proceed systematically across all 369 subjects rather than treating the current subject count as evidence of curriculum completeness.
