## SKA flagship expansion

The structured subject `ska_business_ai_autonomous_operations` now contains 160 lessons: 8 levels with 20 lessons per level. The additional lessons extend foundations, strategy, sales, marketing, finance/operations, technology/AI, autonomous operations, and research/mastery.

## Quality-control direction

The curriculum now needs two distinct completion measurements:

1. **Knowledge depth** — textbook-standard content for each subject.
2. **Curriculum structure** — lesson progression, prerequisites, assessments, study items, and mastery pathways.

A calculated target such as `subjects × 8 × 10` must not be reported as populated lessons unless actual lesson records exist.

## Public/private boundary

Only educational content belongs in this repository. Learner accounts, scores, mastery records, certificates, CRM records, payments, authentication, deployment credentials, and private platform state remain outside the public curriculum corpus.
