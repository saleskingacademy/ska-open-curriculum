# Sales Foundations — Buyer Journey, Funnel Denominators and Integrity

## Lesson
Sales begins with permission, customer problem definition, accurate representation of product capabilities, and a mutually understood next action. A sales funnel is a measurement model, not evidence that one action caused another. Distinguish unique leads, conversations, demonstrations, offers and purchases; define the denominator and observation period before comparing rates. A buyer who does not need the product should be disqualified respectfully.

### Case 1: stage conversion and denominators
A fictional company receives 240 unique inquiries in April. 72 consent to a conversation, 24 attend a demonstration, and 6 purchase. Inquiry-to-conversation conversion is 72/240=30%. Conversation-to-demo conversion is 24/72=33.33%. Demo-to-purchase conversion is 6/24=25%. Inquiry-to-purchase conversion is 6/240=2.5%. Multiplying stage rates (.30×1/3×.25)=.025, consistent with 2.5%. This equality assumes a nested cohort without duplicate counting or time-window mismatch.

### Case 2: integrity and customer fit
A prospective customer requests a guarantee that a workflow will double sales. The seller has no causal trial data and no authorization to guarantee results. The correct response states the available evidence and uncertainty, offers a measurable pilot if appropriate, and avoids the guarantee. A request to continue marketing after an opt-out is rejected.

## Unique eight-question quiz
1. What is the inquiry-to-conversation rate for 72 conversations from 240 inquiries? **30%**; divide 72 by 240.
2. What is conversation-to-demo conversion for 24 of 72? **33.33%**; 24/72.
3. What is demo-to-purchase conversion for 6 of 24? **25%**.
4. What is end-to-end inquiry-to-purchase conversion? **2.5%**; 6/240.
5. Why can multiplying stage rates be misleading across unrelated monthly cohorts? **Denominators and populations may differ**; stage multiplication assumes nested compatible cohorts.
6. How should an unsupported “double sales” guarantee be handled? **Decline the guarantee and disclose evidence limitations**.
7. What is the ethical action when a prospect states the product is unsuitable? **Respectfully disqualify or refer to an appropriate alternative without pressure**.
8. What must a valid next step contain? **Action, owner, timing and buyer agreement**; it is not inferred from silence.
