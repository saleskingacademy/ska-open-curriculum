## Findings
**F-01 CRITICAL — Inadequate evidence of level completion.** Each file contains one relatively short unit. A single unit, even with an examination, cannot establish full competency coverage for an eight-level Sales curriculum. Required action: map every existing lesson and all expected competencies to levels, then identify missing teaching and assessments before claiming completeness.

**F-02 HIGH — Repeated generic assessment questions.** The Foundations, Prospect Research, Solution Design and Advanced Account Strategy units share a common eight-question pattern on unverified estimates, outcomes, causality, opt-outs, agent signatures, contribution and transfer. This provides limited subject-specific discrimination and risks assessment contamination. Required action: replace generic items with unique, level-specific questions, distractors, scenario-based assessments and independently checked answer rationales.

**F-03 HIGH — Insufficient worked practice.** Units of 616–1,186 words provide examples and short exercises but do not demonstrate comprehensive guided-to-independent practice for all stated competencies. Required action: multiple progressively difficult worked examples, common errors, diagnostic checks and unseen transfer cases per competency.

**F-04 HIGH — Source traceability and independent review pending.** Several files lack a dedicated reference framework. None of the eight is independently certified for external factual claims, instructional validity, originality or accessibility. Required action: add authoritative source mapping for material claims and documented review receipts.

**F-05 HIGH — Assessment integrity unproven.** Answer keys are embedded alongside prompts, suitable for instructor material but not independently secure learner testing. No evidence of held-out human or AI-agent evaluation, item analysis, pass-rate validation or remediation effectiveness. Required action: separate instructor and learner variants, maintain protected assessment sets, and test actual learner performance.

**F-06 MEDIUM — Arithmetic spot checks pass.** Confirmed: 80→12→3 gives 15%, 25%, 3.75%; 30/150=20%, 9/150=6%, 9/30=30%; 1,800−700=1,100; 12,000−7,000=5,000 and 15,000−12,000=3,000; 20% of 2,499 discount yields 1,999.20, contribution after 900 cost 1,099.20 and 12-month difference 5,997.60; 40×500=20,000, churn 2,000, expansion 3,000, contraction 1,000, NRR 100%, logo retention 90%; weighted pipeline 2,000+8,000+9,000+4,000=23,000; Brier score (.04+.36+.16+.64)/4=.30. These checks do not establish all quantitative examples are correct.

**F-07 HIGH — Research prompts do not equal postdoctoral instruction.** The advanced units list research extensions but do not provide a complete sequence of advanced theory, research methodology, independent studies, evaluation and peer review. Required action: define the intended level-specific scope and assess it against evidence-based mastery criteria.
