# Sales — Solution Design, Proposals and Acceptance Tests

## Lesson
A proposal is a testable agreement on scope and assumptions. It should include a problem statement, evidence baseline, deliverables, exclusions, acceptance tests, customer responsibilities, implementation dependencies, timeline, pricing, privacy/security conditions, and authorized sign-off. An illustrative ROI scenario is not a guaranteed outcome.

### Worked case
A fictional customer considers a 3-month pilot. Monthly fee is $1,800, direct monthly delivery cost $700, and one-time implementation cost $900. Three-month revenue = $5,400. Three-month direct recurring cost = $2,100. Contribution before implementation = $3,300; after the one-time implementation cost = $2,400. If the pilot produces estimated customer benefits of $2,000/month, gross estimated benefits over three months are $6,000; subtracting the $5,400 customer fees yields $600 estimated net benefit before the customer's internal costs and any one-time customer expenses. The $6,000 figure must not be presented as causally proven without an appropriate comparison.

### Acceptance test
A customer wants median first-response time reduced from 12 hours to 4 hours within 60 days. Define the eligible inquiry population, timezone, working-hours treatment, median calculation, data retention, measurement owner and permitted access. A passing result needs verified measurements, not an agent's assertion.

## Eight distinct quiz questions with solutions
1. What is pilot revenue for three months at $1,800? **$5,400**.
2. What is direct recurring cost at $700 for three months? **$2,100**.
3. What is contribution before implementation? **$3,300**.
4. What is contribution after a $900 implementation cost? **$2,400**.
5. What is estimated customer net benefit from $6,000 benefits and $5,400 fees before other costs? **$600**.
6. Why does an estimated benefit not prove ROI? **Causality, baseline and omitted costs remain unverified**.
7. What is an acceptance criterion? **A measurable, agreed condition with method, owner and timeframe**.
8. Who may approve binding terms? **A properly authorized signatory or delegated approver, not an unapproved agent**.

## Applied examination (100 points)
A. Show all four provider financial calculations with units and period (40 points).
B. Write an acceptance test for median first-response time, including population, baseline, target, period and measurement method (30 points; 6 each).
C. Identify three exclusions/dependencies that must be disclosed in the pilot proposal (30 points; 10 each). **Model answers:** customer CRM access/authorization; excluded data migration; third-party API availability and fees. Other material, relevant disclosures accepted.
**Pass:** ≥85/100 and no unsupported guarantee or unauthorized contract action. **Reassessment:** Monthly price $2,000, monthly direct cost $800, two-month pilot, one-time implementation cost $500.
