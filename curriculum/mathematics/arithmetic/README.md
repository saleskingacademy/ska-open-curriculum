## Audit findings from the 2026-10-03 UTC inspection

The branch initially contained 1,527 whitespace-delimited words in the Lessons 1–5 draft (recalculate with the manifest tool for the authoritative count). The map contains repeated required-pattern text rather than completed teaching for Lessons 6–80. The legacy Arithmetic JSON actually contains one lesson although its metadata plans 80. Its accreditation and percentage-exceedance claims have been replaced with a truthful non-accreditation boundary; its instructional text and quiz are preserved.

The legacy study file includes awkward generated prompts and advanced number-theory material embedded under Arithmetic. Those records are not validated formative assessments for this path. They remain preserved pending item-level review. The generic mathematics lesson dataset states the fundamental theorem of arithmetic for “every integer”; the correct elementary statement concerns integers greater than one, with uniqueness up to order. That separate dataset remains a recorded cross-file remediation issue rather than silently serving as verified prerequisite material.

Lessons 1–5 introduce signed operations, decimals, and division-by-zero reasoning before their full procedural lessons. Such previews require explanation and cannot be prerequisites assumed mastered. New lessons use concrete models and explicitly identify previews. The companions now supply the earlier lesson-package gaps; a course-wide prerequisite and independent academic review is still required before calling Level 1 validated.

## Assessment and validation policy

The map's stricter routine threshold of 85% applies, along with 80% for transfer and correction of failed prerequisite skills. Ten-item sets therefore require at least nine correct. Lesson quizzes provide explanation credit and require 80%. Major tests additionally track critical outcomes. Human explanation may be oral, written, tactile, or diagrammatic; mathematical expectations stay fixed.

Every new lesson is original instructional expression about established mathematics. No accreditation or independently reviewed academic equivalence is claimed. Education-16 and Symbol256 remain deferred. Automated exact-answer checks supplement a source review; they do not prove educational completeness or replace a human academic review. The manifest reports these limits explicitly.

The [Level 1 examination](assessments/level_01_foundation_examination.md) has two 25-item forms with a shared outcome blueprint, explanatory keys, critical-skill thresholds and remediation. The foundational investigation in Lesson 10 is separate from the still-pending Arithmetic course capstone.
