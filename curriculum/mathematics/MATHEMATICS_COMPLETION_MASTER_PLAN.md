Build the mathematics catalogue as a connected zero-to-frontier education system rather than isolated textbooks. A learner may begin with no assumed arithmetic knowledge and progress through undergraduate, graduate, computational, AI-augmented and research-literacy mathematics.

## Scale
The programme is expected to reach millions of words across mathematics when fully populated. Volume is not a completion criterion by itself. There is no fixed word floor per subject. Length follows the declared competency ceiling, instructional needs and assessment evidence; major fields may require multiple volumes. See [the coverage-based policy](../../standards/COVERAGE_BASED_COMPLETION_POLICY.md). Repetition, filler and mechanically inflated prose do not count toward coverage.

## Dependency spine
Numeracy -> Arithmetic -> Pre-Algebra -> Algebra I -> Geometry -> Algebra II -> Trigonometry -> Precalculus -> Calculus I -> Calculus II -> Multivariable Calculus.

From that spine, learners branch into:
- Linear Algebra -> Numerical Linear Algebra -> Functional Analysis / ML mathematics;
- Discrete Mathematics -> Logic -> Computability -> SAT/SMT/Formal Methods;
- Probability -> Statistics -> Stochastic Processes -> Bayesian/ML mathematics;
- Calculus -> Differential Equations -> Dynamical Systems / Scientific Computing;
- Algebra -> Abstract Algebra -> Commutative Algebra -> Algebraic Geometry / Algebraic Number Theory;
- Analysis -> Measure Theory -> Functional/Harmonic Analysis;
- Geometry -> Topology -> Differential Geometry / Algebraic Topology;
- Optimization -> Operations Research / Control / ML optimization.

## Required package per mathematical subject
Every mathematical subject requires definitions, intuition, notation, theorem statements, derivations/proofs appropriate to level, worked examples, guided exercises, independent exercises, mixed review, applications, computational exercises, common-error analysis, quizzes, module exams, cumulative exams, verified solutions, projects/capstones, prerequisite remediation and an AI-verification component at advanced levels.

## Testing progression
Early mathematics emphasizes conceptual understanding and procedural fluency. Intermediate mathematics adds multi-step modeling and transfer. Undergraduate mathematics adds proof and abstraction. Graduate mathematics adds theorem synthesis, advanced methods, literature/research literacy and open-ended problems. Computational/AI-augmented mathematics adds implementation, solver/tool use, verification, numerical reliability, provenance and independent checking of machine-generated reasoning.

## Completion state labels
SKELETON: topic exists.
SEQUENCED: complete prerequisite/module/lesson map exists.
INSTRUCTIONALLY POPULATED: lesson study material and exercises exist.
ASSESSED: quizzes/tests/finals/capstone and solutions exist.
VALIDATED: taxonomy, solutions, prerequisites and assessment alignment pass review.
COMPLETE: all previous gates pass and intended zero-to-ceiling competencies are covered.
