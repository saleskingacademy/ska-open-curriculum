## Purpose
This curriculum assumes no prior arithmetic knowledge and builds toward advanced computational and AI-augmented arithmetic. It is original SKA instructional organization and wording. Mathematical facts, notation and standard methods remain common mathematical knowledge.

## Mastery policy
A learner advances only after demonstrating at least 85% on routine work, 80% on unfamiliar transfer problems, and correction of every missed prerequisite skill. Each lesson requires explanation in the learner's own words, worked examples, independent practice, an error-analysis item and a short assessment. Module mastery adds a cumulative mixed problem set. The final capstone requires independent calculation plus verification of machine-assisted results.
