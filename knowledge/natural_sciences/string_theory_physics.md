# String Theory Physics

## Motivation

Unifying general relativity (gravity, large scale) with quantum mechanics (subatomic). Both work perfectly in their domain but are mathematically incompatible. String theory is the leading candidate for "theory of everything." BASIC IDEA: Fundamental entities are not point particles but 1-dimensional vibrating strings. Different vibration modes = different particles. Like guitar strings — different frequencies produce different notes (particles). String length ~10^-35 m (Planck length). DIMENSIONS: Requires 10 or 11 spacetime dimensions (9 or 10 spatial + time). Extra dimensions compactified (curled up at Planck scale) in Calabi-Yau manifolds. Our 3+1 dimensions are the "visible" brane. FIVE THEORIES → M-THEORY: 5 consistent string theories (Type I, IIA, IIB, Heterotic SO(32), Heterotic E8×E8) unified by Ed Witten (1995) into M-theory in 11 dimensions. SUPERSYMMETRY (SUSY): Every particle has a superpartner (electron ↔ selectron, quark ↔ squark, photon ↔ photino). None found at LHC (yet) — pushes SUSY breaking scale higher. LANDSCAPE: 10^500 possible vacuum states (string landscape). Anthropic principle debate — are we in one of many possible universes? Multiverse implications. ACHIEVEMENTS: Black hole entropy (Strominger-Vafa 1996 — matched Bekenstein-Hawking formula exactly). AdS/CFT correspondence (Maldacena 1997 — holographic principle, gravity in bulk = QFT on boundary). Applications to condensed matter, fluid dynamics. CRITICISM: No experimental predictions testable at achievable energies. Falsifiability debate. "Not even wrong" (Woit). Alternatives: Loop quantum gravity, causal set theory, asymptotic safety. APPLIED: Whether or not string theory is "true," it has produced powerful mathematical tools used across physics.

## Foundations

String theory physics is a theoretical framework in physical sciences that attempts to reconcile quantum mechanics and general relativity. **Quantum mechanics** is a branch of physics that describes the behavior of matter and energy at the smallest scales, where **wave-particle duality** and **uncertainty principle** govern the interactions. **General relativity**, on the other hand, is a theory of gravitation that describes the curvature of spacetime caused by massive objects. The core idea of string theory is that the fundamental building blocks of the universe are not point-like particles, but tiny, vibrating **strings**. These strings exist in a space-time with ten dimensions, of which our observable universe has three dimensions of space (**length**, **width**, and **height**) and one dimension of time. The additional six dimensions are **compactified**, or curled up, so tightly that they are not directly observable. The vibrations of the strings give rise to the various **particle species**, such as electrons, photons, and quarks, which are the fundamental constituents of matter and energy. **Supersymmetry** is a key concept in string theory, which proposes the existence of **superpartners** for each particle, differing by half a unit of spin. ## Methods And Frameworks

In string theory physics, several methods and frameworks are employed to describe the behavior of strings and their interactions. The Polyakov action is a fundamental framework used to describe the dynamics of strings, where the string's worldsheet is embedded in a higher-dimensional spacetime. This method is useful for calculating the string's partition function and scattering amplitudes. However, it fails to provide a clear understanding of the string's off-shell behavior. 
The Ramond-Neveu-Schwarz (RNS) formalism is another framework used to describe the supersymmetric strings, where the string's worldsheet supersymmetry is explicitly realized. This method is useful for calculating the string's spectrum and interactions, but it fails to provide a clear understanding of the string's non-perturbative behavior. 
The Dirichlet brane (D-brane) method is used to describe the behavior of open strings ending on higher-dimensional objects called D-branes. This method is useful for calculating the string's scattering amplitudes and interactions with D-branes, but it fails to provide a clear understanding of the string's behavior in the presence of closed strings. 
The AdS/CFT correspondence is a framework used to describe the behavior of strings in anti-de Sitter (AdS) spacetime, where the string theory is dual to a conformal field theory (CFT) living on the boundary of AdS. This method is useful for calculating the string's behavior in strongly coupled regimes, but it fails to provide a clear understanding of the string's behavior in weakly coupled regimes. 
The string field theory (SFT) method is used to describe the off-shell behavior of strings, where the string field is a functional of the string's configuration. This method is useful for calculating the string's off-shell amplitudes and interactions, but it fails to provide a clear understanding of the string's non-perturbative behavior. The Polyakov action is used to calculate the string's trajectory and vibrations, and is applicable when studying the dynamics of individual strings. The Dirichlet brane approach is utilized to describe the behavior of open strings and their interactions with D-branes, and is particularly useful when analyzing the properties of black holes and the behavior of strings in compactified spaces. The AdS/CFT correspondence is a framework used to study the behavior of strings in anti-de Sitter spaces and their dual conformal field theories, and is applicable when investigating the properties of strongly coupled systems. The string theory landscape is a framework used to describe the vast number of possible string theory vacua, and is relevant when discussing the cosmological implications of string theory. Failure modes of these methods include the difficulty in making precise predictions using the Polyakov action due to the complexity of string interactions, the limitations of the Dirichlet brane approach in describing the behavior of closed strings, and the challenges in extracting physical predictions from the AdS/CFT correspondence due to the complexity of the dual field theories.
