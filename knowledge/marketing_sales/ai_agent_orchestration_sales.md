The extracted insights are used to create Customer Profiles, which are then used to determine the most effective sales strategies. 
The AI agent orchestration system uses a Decision Engine to evaluate the customer profiles and select the most suitable sales approach, such as email, phone call, or social media outreach. Once the sales strategy is determined, the AI agent orchestration system triggers the execution of specific sales tasks, such as sending personalized emails or making automated phone calls. Throughout this process, the AI agent orchestration system maintains a detailed record of all sales interactions, including customer responses, preferences, and outcomes. Use MAPE-K when designing autonomous systems that require continuous monitoring and adaptation. Its failure mode occurs when the knowledge base is incomplete or outdated, leading to suboptimal planning and execution. 
The SOA (Service-Oriented Architecture) framework is applied when integrating multiple AI agents as services, enabling loose coupling and scalability. Use SOA when the system requires flexibility and reconfigurability. Its failure mode arises from inadequate service definition, resulting in integration challenges and reduced system cohesion. 
The Contract Net Protocol (CNP) is utilized for task allocation and negotiation among AI agents, facilitating distributed problem-solving. Apply CNP when the system involves multiple agents with varying capabilities and preferences. Its failure mode occurs when the negotiation process is inefficient, leading to delays and reduced system performance.
