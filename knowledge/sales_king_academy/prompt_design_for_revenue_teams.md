# Prompt Design For Revenue Teams

## Overview

Prompt Design for Revenue Teams teaches how to write instructions that make AI produce reliable, on-brand output for sales and marketing work, and how to turn good prompts into shared team assets.

## Level 1-2: Foundations

A strong prompt states the role, the task, the context, the constraints and the output format. For example: act as a sales development representative, write a first email to this prospect, use these three facts about their company, keep it under 120 words, and end with one question.

Context is the most important ingredient. The model cannot know your product, your customer or your tone unless you give it that information in the prompt or through connected data.

Examples teach style faster than descriptions. Showing two emails that performed well is more effective than describing the style in adjectives.

## Level 3-4: How It Works

A prompt template is a reusable prompt with blanks for the parts that change, such as prospect name, industry and pain point. Templates make output consistent across a team.

Constraints reduce errors. Instructions such as "only use facts from the notes below" and "if a fact is missing, say it is missing" lower the chance of invented details.

Asking the model to reason in steps, such as first listing the prospect's likely priorities and then writing the email, often improves quality on complex tasks.

## Level 5-6: Implementation

Build a prompt library for your team: prospecting emails, follow-ups after calls, objection responses, call summaries and proposal sections. Store each prompt with an owner, a version and an example of good output.

Test prompts like code. Keep a set of sample inputs and the expected qualities of the output, and re-run them whenever the prompt or the model changes.

Protect the brand with a style guide inside the prompt: words to use, words to avoid, reading level and formatting rules.

## Level 7-8: Mastery and Strategy

At mastery level, prompts become part of automated systems. The prompt is combined with data retrieved from the CRM, the output is checked by rules or a second model, and only then is it sent or saved.

Guard against prompt injection, which is text inside customer content that tries to change the model's instructions. Treat any content from outside the company as data, never as instructions.

## Common Mistakes

Vague prompts produce generic output. Specific context about the buyer and the offer is what makes AI writing sound human and relevant.

Letting every rep write their own prompts from scratch creates inconsistent quality. Share and improve the best prompts centrally.

## Hands-On Project

Write a prompt template for a post-call follow-up email that uses the call notes, the next step agreed and your brand style. Test it on five real calls, compare the drafts with what the reps actually sent, and revise the template twice.
