# Real subjects: the SKA catalogue as full textbooks

Basis: UNESCO ISCED-F 2013, the international classification of fields of education (11 broad fields, 29 narrow fields, about 80 detailed fields).
The catalogue's 1,117 topics are grouped into **369 real subjects**. Each real subject is one SKA textbook (at least 120,000 words, 12+ chapters); each existing topic becomes a chapter. Edit `tools/real_subjects.py` to move a topic; per-subject detail is in `map/real_subjects.json`.

Words today: 4,290,895. Words still needed for full textbooks: 40,021,105.
