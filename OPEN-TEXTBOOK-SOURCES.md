# Openly licensed textbook sources (verified 2026-09-28)

`tools/openstax_books.json` lists 21 OpenStax textbooks (~5,000 pages) whose archived
editions are licensed **CC BY 4.0** (commercial use allowed), verified against the
OpenStax content archive. Newer OpenStax editions are CC BY-NC-SA and are NOT used,
because Sales King Academy is a commercial platform.
