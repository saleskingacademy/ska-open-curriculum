# SKA Exclusive Subject: Business, AI & Autonomous Operations

This is a Sales King Academy exclusive interdisciplinary subject built from the combined scope of the SKA business/AI/autonomous-operations book program. It is intentionally larger than a conventional business book: it functions as a structured academic/professional discipline with eight mastery levels, application work, assessments, and future expansion into specialized tracks.

## Eight-level progression

1. Foundations
2. Business Strategy
3. Sales and Revenue
4. Marketing, Product, and Customer
5. Finance, Operations, and Governance
6. Technology, AI, and Automation
7. Autonomous Operations
8. Research, Design, and Mastery

This corpus is original SKA instructional material. It teaches established business, technical, and academic concepts without claiming ownership of underlying facts, theories, or methods.

## Scope expansion

The subject is designed to absorb and extend the material planned for the SKA business/AI book projects. The books become source architecture and intellectual scope; the curriculum turns that scope into sequenced lessons, practice, assessment, projects, specialization, and research-level extensions.

Current release: 80 lessons. This is the first structured release, not the final size. Future releases can expand each level into multiple units and substantially increase instructional depth.
