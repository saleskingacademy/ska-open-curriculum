MARKETING — COMPLETE CURRICULUM

FOUNDATIONS: Marketing is the process of identifying, creating, communicating, and delivering value to customers profitably. It's not just promotion — it's understanding customer needs deeply enough to build and position offerings they want. Core: the right product, to the right people, with the right message, at the right time and price.

THE 4 Ps (Marketing Mix): Product (features, quality, design, brand that meet needs), Price (value-based, cost-plus, competitive, penetration, or skimming), Place (distribution channels — where/how customers buy), Promotion (advertising, PR, sales, digital). Modern extensions add People, Process, Physical evidence (7 Ps for services).

SEGMENTATION, TARGETING, POSITIONING (STP): Segment the market (demographic, geographic, psychographic, behavioral). Target the segments you can serve best and profitably. Position your brand distinctly in the target's mind. Positioning is the single most important strategic marketing decision — it defines what you stand for versus alternatives.

CUSTOMER & BUYER PSYCHOLOGY: The customer journey — Awareness, Consideration, Decision, Retention, Advocacy. The marketing funnel (TOFU/MOFU/BOFU) maps content to journey stage. Understand jobs-to-be-done: customers "hire" products to make progress in their lives. Emotion and identity drive brand choice more than features.

BRAND & POSITIONING: A brand is the sum of perceptions in the customer's mind — the promise you make and keep. Brand equity (awareness, associations, loyalty, perceived quality) is a durable asset. Strong brands command premiums, earn loyalty, and reduce acquisition cost. Consistency across every touchpoint builds brand.

DIGITAL MARKETING: SEO (organic search visibility), SEM/PPC (paid search), content marketing (attract via valuable content), social media (engagement and reach), email marketing (highest ROI channel), influencer and affiliate. The digital funnel is measurable end-to-end — attribution connects spend to revenue.

METRICS & ROI: Key metrics — CAC (Customer Acquisition Cost), LTV (Lifetime Value), LTV:CAC ratio (target 3:1+), conversion rate, ROAS (Return on Ad Spend), churn, MRR/ARR. Marketing must tie to revenue. Measure what matters; vanity metrics (likes, impressions) mislead without conversion.

CONTENT & INBOUND: Inbound marketing attracts customers by creating valuable content matched to their needs, versus interruptive outbound. Content builds trust, authority, and organic reach. The flywheel — attract, engage, delight — turns customers into promoters who fuel growth.

POSITIONING & MESSAGING: A great value proposition states who it's for, what problem it solves, and why it's better/different. Messaging hierarchy — one core promise, supported by proof points. Differentiation must be real and defensible, not just claimed.

MASTERY LEVELS: L1 — understand the 4 Ps and STP. L2-3 — build campaigns and brand positioning. L4-5 — orchestrate multi-channel strategy and measure ROI. L6-7 — lead marketing organizations and growth engines. L8 Grandmaster — teach marketing and build category-defining brands.
