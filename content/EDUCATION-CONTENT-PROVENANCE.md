# Sales King Academy — Original Education Content

## Purpose
This directory contains curriculum material commissioned for Sales King Academy LLC.

## Provenance rule
New lesson copy added by the education-expansion pipeline is written as original instructional material for SKA. It is not copied from textbooks, publisher courseware, paid courses, or third-party curriculum packages.

The curriculum may teach facts, theories, formulas, methods, historical events, and other public-domain or commonly taught subject matter. Those underlying facts and ideas are not themselves claimed as proprietary. SKA's original expression, organization, sequencing, exercises, assessments, metadata, and curriculum architecture are treated as SKA-authored material to the extent permitted by applicable law.

## Copyright limitation
Custom or AI-assisted generation does not automatically guarantee copyright protection for every word. Copyright protection depends on applicable law and the degree of human authorship. SKA should retain human editorial control, substantially revise and curate generated material, and maintain provenance records for important releases.

## Quality standard
Each subject is organized across eight mastery levels:
1. Foundations
2. Core Concepts
3. Intermediate Application
4. Advanced Undergraduate
5. Honors / Advanced
6. Graduate Study
7. Doctoral Preparation
8. Research & Frontier

A lesson should support the sequence:
Learn → Practice → Demonstrate → Test → Remediate → Retest → Master → Apply → Synthesize.
