# Archived unfinished lesson drafts

The 800 lessons archived under `content/quarantine/template-lessons/` are
unchanged original records from `content/lessons/`. They began with the
generic boilerplate “This original Sales King Academy lesson develops…”,
contained template-only worked examples, and must not be represented as
completed instructional content.

The earlier `content/QUARANTINE-placeholder-lessons.json` manifest identified
792 of these; a read-only content check identified another eight psychology
templates. The manifest now records all 800 template lesson IDs, in addition
to 388 other quarantined wrong-subject-ID records. None of the original
lesson content is deleted from repository history or from this archive.

Only restore an item to the published `content/lessons/` directory after
it has substantive instruction specific to its subject, a verified worked
example, exercises, assessments, and human editorial review. The
subject-integrity CI guard should fail on accidental template republishing.
